interests

Outside the lab, I’m either reading, writing poetry, in rehearsal, at a badminton court, or underwater with a robot.

music

  • I've been playing music since I was 4 and music has always been one of my favourite ways to express myself
  • At school, I play flute as first chair in the philharmonic orchestra and senior chamber ensemble, and I self-taught myself how to play bass for the senior rock band!
  • What I love most, however, is to grow the music community at my school and to help younger students develop a lifelong passion for music through being a music ambassador at my school's music advisory council, where we plan events like lunch-time concerts to engage the school community in music events, advise the music department on logistics and operations, and help run large-scale Christmas, Lunar New Year, spring and summer recitals.

badminton

  • Fun fact: I used to be a professional badminton player at the HK junior national team!
  • After ending my professional career due to injury, I now lead my school's varsity badminton team in inter-school games, acting as captain for the U14s.
  • My role includes leading training drills, coordinating logistics between school coaches and players, and everything in between from training our team's soft skills, technical skills and morale
  • Overall, I love the badminton community both in and out of school so much, and the grit and tenacity I've gained through playing sports have followed through every other sidequest I'ved pursued.

reading

  • Whenever I have any free time, you'll always find me in a window seat at my favourite cafes (shoutout to Preface Coffee and Halfway Coffee) with a good book and a matcha to go with it
  • I love reading paperbacks, making lots of annotations and notes while I read, and writing excessively lengthy reviews/rants on Goodreads
  • You'll always find me either in a reading slump or a reading marathon with no in between
  • Some of my favourites include Ocean Vuong, Murakami, Kazuo Ishiguro, RF Kuang, and of course the classic Jane Austen, and I'm always up for historical fiction, poetry or (auto-)biographies
