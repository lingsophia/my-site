writing

I’ve been a reporter at the South China Morning Post, the largest English newspaper in Hong Kong, since May 2023. Over the past 3 years, I’ve published 50+ articles in the youth column YoungPost, including front-page headlines, opinion pieces on social issues in Hong Kong and pop culture reviews, reaching readers at over 85% of schools in Hong Kong.

I also write and edit for the Youth Journalism International, an international jouranlism nonprofit offering free journalism education to students from all backgrounds, where I mentor students from different countries across the world in journalism skills and offer editorial advice.
